Planning

Projects often begin long before production. Phinneywood is designed to support ideation, preproduction, technical planning, design development, and collaborative exploration.

Creative development, production meetings, workshops, and technical planning.
Space for drawing, layout, system design, budgeting, and experimentation.
Built to support both artistic direction and practical execution.

Fabrication

The studio supports the physical side of production as well: fabrication, engineering, electronics, scenic work, technical integration, and custom-built systems.

Furniture, scenic construction, electronics, control systems, and installations.
Technical infrastructure designed for reliability, adaptability, and repeated use.
Creative and engineering work developed side-by-side.

Rehearsal

Practice and iteration are treated as central creative activities. The space is intended to support rehearsal, refinement, experimentation, classes, and collaborative development over time.

Music rehearsals, staging, workshops, classes, and production development.
Flexible layouts designed to evolve with the work being created.
A place for repeated use, adaptation, and long-term process.

Production

Phinneywood supports filming, live performance, installations, and public presentation with integrated technical systems and practical production infrastructure.

Camera, grip, lighting, sound, power, staging, and technical direction.
Support for shoots, performances, screenings, presentations, and gatherings.
Designed to move fluidly between closed development and public-facing work.

Post

The work continues after production. Editing, sound, music, finishing, review, and reflection are treated as part of the same creative arc rather than separate downstream steps.

Editing, review, music, sound, finishing, and collaborative postproduction.
Support for long-form development and iterative refinement.
A continuous environment for work from concept through completion.

Creative and technical work for film, performance, and live environments.
